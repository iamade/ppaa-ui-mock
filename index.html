PeopleProtocol.ca — agency flow

We design, build, operate, QA, and improve business-specific AI systems.

An agency pipeline, not a tool rental: intake → scope → plan → your approval → build → QA → your approval → live. This mock shows the client intake and the agency-side operations view.

1

Intake

Structured discovery: business profile, problem, data, systems and constraints — captured once, reused everywhere.

2

Scope & plan

Agents draft scope and a delivery plan. Nothing proceeds past the human gate without your sign-off.

3

Build & QA

Build runs with QA evidence attached — test logs and screenshots — before anything is called done.

4

Approve & go live

You approve in the app after walking the running link. Only then does it ship.

5

Operate

Ongoing operation and improvement: health, cost, and drift monitored on one surface.

6

Evidence

Every ticket carries its own proof — comments, logs, links. No silent closures.
